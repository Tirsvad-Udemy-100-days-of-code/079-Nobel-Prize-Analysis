## What the code does

Run `python -m nobel_analysis` to print every answer of the assignment in one report, or open the notebook to see the charts. `python -m pytest` proves each value.

## What you gained

- Review: finding and explaining NaN values; converting objects and strings to numbers; donut and bar charts with plotly; a rolling average for a time series; `.value_counts()`, `.groupby()`, `.merge()`, `.sort_values()` and `.agg()`.
- New: a choropleth map; bar charts of different segments of the data; sunburst charts; Seaborn `.lmplot()` with `row`, `hue` and `lowess`; a different picture emerges when you look at the same data in different ways; the distribution of the data in a Seaborn histogram.
